5 Key Early Warning Signs of Heart Disease in Indian Adults

Written & Reviewed by Dr. Rajesh Sharma
Senior Consultant Cardiologist, AIIMS Alumnus • Updated August 2026

Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of mortality in India, affecting adults at a significantly younger age compared to Western populations. South Asian genetics, combined with sedentary urban lifestyles and metabolic syndromes, make early screening imperative.

1. Unexplained Chest Discomfort or Heavy Pressure

Not all cardiac events manifest as sharp, severe pain. Many patients report a sensation of heavy tightness, squeezing, or fullness in the center of the chest that lasts for more than a few minutes.

2. Shortness of Breath During Routine Activities

If climbing a single flight of stairs or walking a short distance leaves you unusually winded or fatigued, it may indicate reduced pumping efficiency of the heart muscle.

3. Pain Radiating to the Left Arm, Jaw, or Upper Back

Cardiac pain often radiates along nerve pathways to the left arm, neck, jaw, or shoulder blades. This is frequently misattributed to acidity or muscle strain.

Recommended Diagnostic Tests for Heart Health:

  • ✔ Electrocardiogram (ECG) & 2D Echo Color Doppler
  • ✔ Lipid Profile & High-Sensitivity CRP (hs-CRP)
  • ✔ Treadmill Stress Test (TMT)
