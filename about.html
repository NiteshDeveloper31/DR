Redefining Medical Care Across India

AAROGYA ONE was founded in Mumbai Central by a group of AIIMS cardiologists and technology leaders with a single vision: to eliminate long OPD waiting times and give every Indian family direct access to world-class specialists.

Over the past 15 years, we have expanded into 25+ super-specialty medical centers across Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai, treating over 12,000 patients annually with NABH-accredited excellence.
