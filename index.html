Simply select your specialty or doctor, pick a convenient date & time slot, enter patient details, and click confirm. You will receive an instant digital appointment voucher pass on SMS and email.

Yes, all AAROGYA ONE super specialty centers have dedicated TPA insurance desks that support 100% cashless claims with Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Max Bupa, and all PSU insurance providers.

Our digital check-in system guarantees an average wait time of under 4 minutes. Show your QR voucher pass at the OPD desk for priority entry.
