About Dr. Rajesh Sharma

Dr. Rajesh Sharma is one of India's foremost Interventional Cardiologists with over 18 years of clinical excellence in treating complex coronary heart diseases, hypertension, heart failure, and structural cardiac conditions.

Having completed his DM in Cardiology from AIIMS New Delhi, he has performed over 4,500 successful coronary angioplasties and pacemaker implantations. He serves as Chief Cardiac Advisor at AAROGYA ONE Super Specialty Center in Mumbai Central.

Hypertension & Cholesterol Control
